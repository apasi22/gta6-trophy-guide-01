Roadmap to the platinum

Do the stages in order. The missable trophies are the ones to watch — finish them when they appear, because a named story mission or a stranger finale may not come back.

  1. Stage 1 — Story playthrough 13 trophies

    Play Chapters 1–4 and the final mission. Complete every named story mission when it is offered: Gone Fishing, Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child’s Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown and Operation Savage Glory. These are the missable story trophies — do not skip them.

  2. Stage 2 — Strangers & freaks 6 trophies

    Work through the stranger mission strands (question-mark markers): Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, finishing each one’s final mission. Completing 8 full strands also earns Strangers & Freaks. Do the finales before the story ends, while the strangers are still available.

  3. Stage 3 — Collectibles & exploration 8 trophies

    Sweep Leonida region by region: meditation spots, card sets, WAiNK It locations, unique-animal hunting for Bret, 100 vehicles, exploring the whole state, all garage properties, and 10 Scooter Bros registrations. Nothing here is missable — it can all be done in free roam.

  4. Stage 4 — Activities & challenges 26 trophies

    Races, drone races, the shooting range, fishing with Cal, arcade games, bowling, stunts, challenges, robberies, Combat Focus and the situational trophies (4-star escape with your partner, 3 shops back-to-back, the airport departure, the showroom smash). Do the situational ones deliberately — they need the right setup, not luck.

  5. Stage 5 — Cleanup & 100% 2 trophies

    Finish whatever the checklist still shows, then check the in-game stats for 100% completion. A Long Road (100%) pops first; the platinum, Vice Got a Price!, follows automatically once the other 54 are earned.

Missable trophies (22): the 12 named story missions and stranger finales, plus the situational ones — Hot Together, Retail Therapy, Catch Me If You Can, Sneakthief, A Classic Blitz Play, Capital Offense, Actions Speak Louder, Bad Luck I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin. Use the Missable filter to see them all.

About this guide

The guide is a static site: it loads nothing from the network, your progress is saved only in this browser (localStorage), and export/import moves it as a file you own.
